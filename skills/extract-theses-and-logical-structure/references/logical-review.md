# Logical completion and adversarial source review

Read this for every extraction/review. Schema validation checks Horn shape and provenance; it does not show that natural-language premises entail their conclusion.

## Test each clause before adding it

1. Write the exact signed consequent and the independently asserted premises, preserving quantifiers, modalities, referents, time and selected meanings. A norm or general rule can be one atomic proposition; a separately asserted conjunction should be split.
2. Ask: could all these premises hold while this exact consequent fails? State a concrete counterexample privately. Do not assume the clause itself, its explanation, the speaker's authority or the target conclusion when answering.
3. Identify only the missing commitments that exclude that counterexample. Check for missing normative standards, factual applicability, relevant analogy, causal mechanism, generalisation/representativeness and exceptions. Continue until no known counterexample remains under the declared interpretations, or record that the proposed inference cannot be completed responsibly.
4. Search the existing knowledge and whole source context for each commitment. Reuse matching atoms with the same meaning/scope, including cross-session evidence. A passage asserting a nearby concept or reporting an example does not substantiate a stronger bridge.
5. If no source asserts a necessary commitment, apply the induced-premise exception: a minimal, independently contestable assertion; explicit unsubstantiated status; no assertion origins; separately cited reconstruction context and rationale; at least one other genuinely sourced antecedent atom. Mark the repaired clause reconstructed. Do not assert that this supplies truth or group acceptance.
6. Search for support and refutation of the newly induced atom. A source may challenge the bridge even when it never asserts it. Keep such challenges visible on that atom. If genuine substantiation is found, update evidence status rather than leaving a misleading unsubstantiated badge.
7. Read each atom without the clause explanation. Is it one assertion with a clear subject and a conceivable denial? Split independent claims, remove editorial framing and identify referents. Do not shorten by deleting modality, exceptions or morally relevant distinctions. A general norm may be one conditional principle; several independent conditions of a particular case should be separate atoms.
8. Remove every premise in turn. If the remaining premises still establish the same consequent, remove that redundant premise from this clause and keep any sourced statement in its speech history. In particular, a general example, an already-implied weaker statement, and a cause mentioned inside the consequent often add no necessary premise. Do not strengthen another induced premise solely to manufacture dependence on a convenient sourced atom.

Natural-language deduction still requires judgment. Do not present an automated entailment guarantee or repair every clause with a mechanically generated “these premises imply that conclusion” atom. The latter conceals the original gap instead of exposing a meaningful commitment.

Check the completed antecedent again, not only the original gap. A new premise that already asserts the consequent with different wording is not a repair. Do not pad an otherwise all-induced inference with an irrelevant sourced atom to pass validation. Where a completion needs several distinct commitments (for example, institutional authority, available demand and a welfare criterion), expose their separate roles rather than bundling them into a case-specific restatement of the conclusion.

As an additional check, temporarily remove the sourced premises: if the induced premises alone supply the entire claimed inference, look for circular completion or evidentiary padding. A definition or value criterion can legitimately be induced, but it must still require genuine sourced applicability or other source-substantiated content. Schema validation cannot establish this semantic requirement.

When an invalid clause cannot be repaired responsibly, withdraw it from the active argument graph and preserve its full record, occurrences and reason in the private correction history. Retain the original sourced assertions and an explicit private gap note. A gap question may be shown on the website only when the source actually asks it. If an unsubstantiated draft premise loses its only legitimate clause use, archive that draft with the correction rather than keeping a fictitious active use or adding a substitute clause merely to satisfy the schema. Shared premises remain attached to their remaining genuine uses; never delete sourced speech or erase the history of a later-substantiated premise.

Distinguish a report from its content: “a participant believes that P” does not entail P. When the source actually advances P, extract that assertion with its attribution, qualifications and occurrence stance; provenance does not certify its truth. If the source only reports that someone else holds a belief, do not silently upgrade the report. Similarly, a conclusion conditional on a philosophical theory needs an additional commitment to that theory before it can support an unqualified conclusion; preserve unresolved meanings when the speaker's terminology is unclear.

When a definition itself is disputed, distinguish a claim about which definition is adequate from a claim made inside a stipulated definition. Do not make one position tautological by selecting its proposed meaning, then treat a different selected meaning as its logical refutation. Where the source warrants it, represent the adequacy proposal as an explicit proposition and expose the inclusion or evaluation criterion used to challenge it. Preserve both meanings and their source history; if the source does not warrant that reconstruction, retain the semantic disagreement as a question.

Check modal conjunctions and comparisons explicitly. “B is possible” and “C is possible” do not establish that B and C can occur together, in the same people, period or setting. A causal comparison needs comparable cases, not just two differently described outcomes. Distinguish reports, preferences, capabilities, actions and outcomes; an intended effect is not an achieved effect. Extract the narrower attested facts and expose only the missing commitments the particular inference needs.

## Fictional examples

- A = “The courtyard should remain accessible.” B = “Neighbours meet in the courtyard.” B alone has a normative gap. C = “Places that enable neighbourly meetings should remain accessible” supplies the general policy. Search for C. If absent and necessary to this proposed argument, it can be an induced normative premise with no asserted occurrence: B ∧ C ⇒ A. Another participant's fire-safety argument may contest C's lack of exceptions; attach that challenge to C, not automatically to B or A.
- “One garden improved after a grant” does not entail “Every garden improves after grants.” Do not invent universality or silently weaken the existing consequent. A warranted representative/mechanistic premise may complete a qualified conclusion; otherwise preserve the generalisation gap as an open question.
- “Noise can disturb some visitors” does not refute “Some visitors enjoy lively spaces.” Both can hold. It may instead challenge “All visitors enjoy noise.” Canonical polarity and quantifiers decide the target.
- “The north gate is locked” suffices for “At least one gate is locked” in the same setting. One premise is enough here. In contrast, a general rule about what locked gates prevent needs a separate fact establishing that the particular gate is locked. Do not omit applicability or add a premise merely to imitate a two-premise example.
- A source's bare “I disagree” records rejection, not a premise-free refutation. Look for its reason in neighbouring turns and later returns; if absent, preserve the disagreement without inventing a Horn clause.

## Two distinct source passes

Use a private atom ledger with IDs, supporting passage candidates, refuting passage candidates, dispositions and outstanding bridge questions. Search all atoms, including leaves and induced premises, even if their topics are not focal theses. The second pass must actively test opposition rather than relabel results from the support pass.

Read the preceding/following turns for implicit references (“that,” “not necessarily,” counterexamples, concessions). Follow interwoven topics across session boundaries. Distinguish objections to facts, definitions, moral standards, applicability and inference steps. Meaning disagreement often requires separate scoped propositions rather than a false red edge.

For each active clause, retain a concise review record: exact premise IDs, signed consequent, logical role of every bridge, assertion evidence versus inference context, induced/source status, counterexamples checked and resulting corrections. Keep the visible explanation a secondary extraction note; never hide a necessary premise in prose above the atoms.

If an older atom is broader than the actual two-sided exchange, preserve it and introduce or reuse the narrower sourced claim for that exchange. Correct the affected clause targets with a private history. An objection to one proposed intervention must not become a negation of every intervention in its class; a narrow affirmative case may support an appropriately existential broader claim, but that transfer also needs source warrant.

## Preserve competing and independent routes

A graph can legitimately contain `B ∧ C ⇒ A`, `D ∧ E ⇒ A`, `F ∧ G ⇒ ¬A`, and `H ⇒ ¬A` together. The first two are not the single clause `B ∧ C ∧ D ∧ E ⇒ A`; the last two neither erase nor silently weaken A's supports. Independence here means a distinct inferential route, not probabilistic independence. Deduplicate repeated premise sets and preserve shared dependencies.

For a universal or inevitability claim, inspect reported failures as candidate counterexamples. A fictional source might first argue that all marked gates are passable because every marked gate is unlocked and every unlocked gate is passable. Later it reports that the north gate is marked but impassable. The two general premises support the universal claim; the two case facts refute it. Preserve both clauses and source stances, even when one speaker supplied both. The counterexample can also refute the unlocked-to-passable bridge if the source separately establishes that this gate is unlocked; do not infer that applicability fact merely to create a deeper red edge.

A hoped-for benefit, moral preference, or beneficial tendency is not by itself an inevitability argument. Expose an attested or essential induced sufficiency/generalisation commitment where the source warrants that stronger inference; otherwise retain the narrower claim and record the gap. Conversely, do not dismiss an explicitly offered counterexample merely because the earlier assertion was an expectation or the objection was indirect. Check the same outcome, time horizon and exposure conditions. One failed attempt refutes a universal success claim, not every weaker possibility or beneficial tendency.

Run the premise-removal and sourced-premise checks separately for every opposing route. A disputed sourced assertion still counts as source-substantiated: that label records speech provenance, not acceptance or truth. Retire a clause only for an extraction/entailment defect, not because its conclusion or a premise has a credible counterargument.

## Completion criteria

- Every existing and newly added atom has support and refutation search coverage; “none found” includes a reason and source scope, not an inferred consensus.
- Every active clause has a completed logical check, at least one source-substantiated premise, and no concealed bridge.
- Every induced premise is essential to a named clause, independently contestable, visibly unsubstantiated, and not assigned invented assertion provenance or a speaker.
- Repeated rules/evidence do not inflate counts; cycles do not manufacture independent support.
- Simultaneous support/refutation and multiple routes on either side survive whenever warranted; coverage records every candidate's disposition rather than stopping after the first argument or imposing artificial balance.
- Exact saved-record provenance and semantic review both pass, with a private correction history and no claims of human approval.
